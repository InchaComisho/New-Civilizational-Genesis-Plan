## Overview

**New Civilizational Genesis Plan** is a civilization-scale fully circulative infrastructure framework designed to stabilize Earth’s climate and restore ecosystems.

The plan integrates existing technologies to reboot disrupted natural cycles. In this context, rebooting does not mean that humans dominate nature. It means connecting oceans, deserts, cities, soil, microorganisms, water circulation, energy systems, and AI observation so that nature’s original capacities for circulation, metabolism, cooling, and regeneration can be complemented and restored.

This framework is not conventional geoengineering. It does not seek to replace nature with artificial control. Instead, it is based on **Natural Complementation Science**: a method that provides minimal, reversible support to weakened natural functions until nature can regain autonomous circulation.

The plan integrates three major domains:

1. **Ocean Metabolism Reboot**: OBS / Ocean Breathing System
2. **Desert Ecosystem Reboot**: freshwater lakes, artificial seawater lakes, and microbial restoration
3. **Urban Biosphere Revival**: mist cooling, circulative waterways, and distributed energy

These are unified by a civilizational OS called the **Six Principles**: **Natural Law / Providence, Harmony, Circulation, Structure, Order, and Wa**.

## Abstract

The modern climate and ecological crises are not caused by CO₂ emissions alone.

At the deeper level, Earth’s natural circulation systems themselves are stopping or weakening.

- weakening vertical ocean circulation
- oxygen depletion and reduced nutrient transport in the ocean
- collapse of soil microorganisms and humus formation
- desertification and drying
- transformation of cities into heat-death zones
- disruption of organic matter circulation
- extractive design of human infrastructure

These are not separate problems. They are symptoms of planetary metabolic failure.

This plan proposes a transition from civilization as a system that extracts resources to civilization as a system that maintains circulation.

```text
Extraction Civilization
→ Circulation Civilization
→ Planetary Regeneration Civilization
```

Technology alone is not enough.

What is required is a civilizational operating system that can guide technology correctly.

## 1. Background: The Root Cause Is Not CO₂ Alone

Modern climate policy is often framed around emission reduction.

CO₂ reduction is necessary. However, emission reduction alone does not restore Earth’s metabolism.

This plan begins from the following diagnosis:

1. **Natural circulation systems are weakening.**  
   Ocean stratification, reduced vertical convection, soil degradation, urban heat islands, and drying are progressing simultaneously.

2. **Microbial and biological foundations are collapsing.**  
   Soil microorganisms, marine microorganisms, phytoplankton, humus formation, and biodiversity are weakening.

3. **Human infrastructure is not designed to support planetary metabolism.**  
   Modern cities, agriculture, energy systems, and logistics prioritize short-term efficiency and extraction rather than circulation maintenance.

If Earth’s circulation and biological metabolism continue to weaken, the planet can keep destabilizing even while emissions are reduced.

Therefore, civilization must shift from **resource extraction** to **circulation reconstruction**.

## 2. Definitions: Natural Symbiosis Science and Natural Complementation Science

### 2.1 Natural Symbiosis Science

**Natural Symbiosis Science** is an applied science framework that designs human civilization not as a controller outside nature, but as a subsystem participating inside natural cycles.

Conventional ideas of “symbiosis” often still place humans at the center:

- humans as primary beneficiaries
- nature as a managed asset
- conservation as a method for sustained exploitation

In Master’s definition, humans are not owners of Earth. Humans are one subsystem within Earth’s systems and have a responsibility to build structures that do not destroy natural circulation.

Natural Symbiosis Science requires:

- civilization to be structurally compatible with natural circulation
- ecosystems to be treated as the primary operating layer of planetary stability
- humans to accept responsibility as a subsystem of Earth, not rulers of Earth

---

### 2.2 Natural Complementation Science

**Natural Complementation Science** is the engineering branch of Natural Symbiosis Science.

Its core principle is:

> When natural circulation is broken, humans should provide minimal, reversible, controllable assistance that helps nature regain autonomous metabolism.

This is not domination.

It is co-evolutionary intervention: supporting what nature already does — breathing, circulating, cooling, metabolizing, and regenerating.

### Conditions of Natural Complementation Science

- minimal intervention
- reversibility
- continuous observation
- ecological safety constraints
- restoration of autonomous natural function
- avoidance of permanent artificial control

### 5. Order

### 6. Wa

The Six Principles are not decorative ideals.  
They are engineering constraints for civilizational survival.

## 4. System Overview: Planetary Circulation Reboot Framework

This plan is not a collection of isolated technologies. It is an integrated planetary circulation reboot model linking oceans, deserts, cities, energy, water, microorganisms, and AI observation.

---

### 4.1 Ocean Metabolism Reboot: OBS / Ocean Breathing System

#### Problem

Ocean stratification and reduced mixing prevent deep nutrients from reaching the surface layers.  
As a result, phytoplankton productivity, oxygen supply, biological CO₂ fixation, and ocean food webs weaken.

#### Method

- Inject compressed air into deep ocean layers.
- Release it as nano-bubbles.
- Use the rising bubble plume to induce vertical convection.
- Bring deep nutrients into the photic zone.
- Support phytoplankton activity.
- Restore oxygenation and carbon fixation.
- Support localized surface temperature stabilization.

#### Expected Outcomes

- reduction of hypoxic zones
- recovery of plankton-based food webs
- fisheries stabilization
- increase in biological carbon fixation
- climate stabilization support through restored ocean metabolism

---

### 4.2 Desert Ecosystem Reboot: Freshwater Lakes and Artificial Seawater Lakes

#### Problem

Deserts are not merely dry lands.  
Many deserts have lost microorganisms, water-retention layers, vegetation, humidity layers, and biodiversity, becoming biologically stalled landscapes.

#### Method

- Construct freshwater lakes to create humidity, microclimate buffering, and biodiversity bases.
- Construct artificial seawater lakes to introduce marine-type productivity inland.
- Use sealed structures, shallow-zone design, and waterways to manage salinity risk.
- Combine aquaculture, seaweed industries, salt-tolerant vegetation, and controlled mangrove-type zones.
- Connect vegetation belts, waterways, and shade structures to form linear oasis networks.

#### Expected Outcomes

- moderation of day-night temperature extremes
- humidity recovery
- dust storm mitigation
- reboot of microbial and soil ecosystems
- inland aquatic protein production
- conversion of dead land into biosphere belts

---

### 4.3 Urban Biosphere Revival: Mist Cooling, Circulative Waterways, and Distributed Energy

#### Problem

Cities are invisible deserts.

Asphalt, concrete, waste heat, direct sunlight, and dryness can raise urban surface temperatures to 60–70°C. Under these conditions, microorganisms and vegetation cannot survive stably.

#### Method

- Deploy ultrasonic nano-mist cooling.
- Use fine droplets of around 1–10 μm for evaporative cooling without soaking surfaces.
- Place circulative waterways under solar panels.
- Use the same water system for panel cooling, water storage, micro-hydro generation, and thermal stabilization.
- Combine perovskite solar cells, vertical-axis wind turbines, and micro-hydro systems.
- Connect energy, water, and logistics through protected underground corridors.

#### Expected Outcomes

- suppression of urban heat runaway
- reintroduction of microorganisms and green infrastructure
- improvement of energy efficiency
- disaster-resistant self-sustaining urban modules
- formation of a foundation for returning cities to the biosphere
